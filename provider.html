Meet Your Provider

Dr. Mispa Afueh, DNP, CRNP, FNP-BC, RNC-MNN

Doctor of Nursing Practice • Board-Certified Family Nurse Practitioner

Dr. Mispa Afueh, DNP, FNP-BC, RNC-MNN, is a Doctor of Nursing Practice and Board-Certified Family Nurse Practitioner with more than 17 years of nursing and advanced practice experience devoted to advancing women's health. She founded All About Women's Health LLC with a clear mission: to deliver exceptional, evidence-based care that empowers women to achieve lifelong health and wellness.

Known for her compassionate, patient-centered approach, Dr. Afueh provides comprehensive care across every stage of a woman's life; from adolescence through menopause and beyond. She believes every woman deserves personalized, respectful, and culturally responsive healthcare built on trust, education, and shared decision-making. By combining advanced clinical expertise with genuine compassion, she partners with each patient to promote prevention, early detection, informed choices, and lasting well-being.

At All About Women's Health LLC, every woman is welcomed with dignity, listened to with compassion, and cared for with clinical excellence.
