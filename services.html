Preventive & Wellness

Preventive Care & Annual Exams

Clinical Overview

Preventive health care is the cornerstone of lifelong wellness. Our comprehensive well-woman evaluations assess health risk factors, deliver thorough screenings, and establish proactive health management plans tailored to your life stage.

Clinical Scope & Diagnostic Procedures
  • Annual Well-Woman Physical Examinations
  • Pap Smears & Cervical Cancer Screening (HPV Co-testing)
  • Clinical Breast Exams & Digital Mammogram Referrals
  • Cardiovascular, Thyroid & Metabolic Risk Screening
Who May Benefit: All women ages 13 and older seeking routine annual evaluations and proactive disease prevention.
Key Clinical Benefit: Early detection of gynecological abnormalities and long-term health risk reduction.
Gynecology

Gynecologic Care & Evaluations

Clinical Overview

Expert clinical evaluation and diagnostic management for acute and chronic gynecological concerns. We provide compassionate, evidence-based solutions in a gentle, non-judgmental environment.

Clinical Scope & Diagnostic Procedures
  • Gynecologic Evaluations & Chronic Pelvic Pain Assessment
  • Vaginal Infection & Recurrent UTI Treatment
  • Confidential STI Screening, Testing & Targeted Treatment
  • PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) Diagnosis & Management
  • Endometriosis Evaluation & Multidisciplinary Referral
Who May Benefit: Women experiencing pelvic pain, irregular cycles, symptoms of infection, or hormonal disorders.
Key Clinical Benefit: Rapid symptom relief, clear diagnostic answers, and individualized treatment protocols.
Reproductive Health

Family Planning & Contraception

Clinical Overview

Empowering women with modern contraceptive technologies and comprehensive family planning counseling. We guide you toward the method best suited for your body, health profile, and reproductive timeline.

Clinical Scope & Diagnostic Procedures
  • Contraceptive Counseling (Pills, Patches, Vaginal Rings)
  • In-Office IUD Insertion & Removal (Mirena, Kyleena, Paragard)
  • Nexplanon Subdermal Arm Implant Placement & Removal
  • Preconception Counseling & Fertility Evaluation Referrals
Who May Benefit: Women seeking reliable long-acting reversible contraception (LARC) or preconception care.
Key Clinical Benefit: Highly effective, personalized birth control tailored to your reproductive goals.
Obstetrics

Pregnancy Care & Postpartum Support

Clinical Overview

Nurturing expectant mothers through every phase of pregnancy and early motherhood. Leveraging specialized Maternal-Newborn Nursing (RNC-MNN) expertise, we deliver supportive prenatal and postpartum care.

Clinical Scope & Diagnostic Procedures
  • Pregnancy Confirmation & Gestational Clinical Assessment
  • Trimester Prenatal Milestone Care & Nutritional Counseling
  • Postpartum Physical Recovery Care & Lactation Guidance
  • Postpartum Mood Disorder & Emotional Health Screening
Who May Benefit: Expectant mothers seeking continuous maternal guidance and postpartum wellness.
Key Clinical Benefit: Specialized maternal-newborn nursing expertise ensuring maternal and infant health.
Menopause & Hormones

Hormonal Health & Menopause (BHRT)

Clinical Overview

Comprehensive evaluation and management of hormonal shifts, perimenopause transitions, and post-menopausal health. We restore vitality through customized bioidentical therapy and bone density protection.

Clinical Scope & Diagnostic Procedures
  • Perimenopause & Menopause Symptom Management
  • Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT)
  • Osteoporosis Risk Evaluation & DEXA Scan Guidance
  • Hot Flash, Insomnia & Mood Shift Clinical Therapies
Who May Benefit: Women navigating hot flashes, sleep disturbances, fatigue, or menopause-related changes.
Key Clinical Benefit: Rebalanced hormone levels, improved quality of life, and bone density preservation.
Adolescent Health

Adolescent Gynecology (Ages 13+)

Clinical Overview

Dedicated, age-appropriate gynecological care for young women ages 13 and older. We focus on education, building comfort, and establishing healthy habits in a gentle, reassuring atmosphere.

Clinical Scope & Diagnostic Procedures
  • First Gynecological Visits & Pubertal Health Education
  • Irregular Period Evaluation & Menstrual Cramp Relief
  • HPV Vaccination (Gardasil 9) Counseling
  • Adolescent Reproductive Wellness & Hygiene Guidance
Who May Benefit: Young women ages 13 and up establishing initial reproductive health care.
Key Clinical Benefit: Supportive, comfortable introduction to lifelong reproductive health and body confidence.
