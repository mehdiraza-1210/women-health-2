Frequently Asked Questions

Click on a question below to reveal detailed answers:

What should I expect during my first well-woman visit? +
Your initial visit includes a comprehensive medical history review, discussion of personal health concerns, and necessary clinical screenings in a supportive, respectful environment. We prioritize listening and empowering you to lead your health choices.
At what age should a young woman start gynecological care? +
We welcome patients starting at age 13. Initial adolescent visits focus primarily on health education, period irregularity management, and building comfort with your provider, without mandatory internal exams unless clinically indicated.
What insurance plans are accepted at your practice? +
We accept major commercial health insurance networks, Medicaid, and Medicare plans across Maryland, Washington DC, and Virginia. Contact our billing staff for specific coverage confirmation.
How do I request prescription refills or access lab test results? +
Prescription refills and lab results are securely managed through our clinical team. You can call our office directly or submit a message through our online contact form.
